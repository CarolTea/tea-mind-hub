

# Tea Mind Business Hub — Homepage

## Overview
A luxurious, institutional homepage for Tea Mind Business Hub — a strategic business platform for the tea industry. The design follows a "fintech meets luxury editorial" aesthetic: structured, confident, minimal, and modern.

## Visual Identity
- **Palette**: Off-white (#F3EFE7) background, Deep Green (#1C2B22), Midnight Blue (#0F1A2B), Strategic Gold (#C6A75E) used sparingly
- **Typography**: Playfair Display for headlines, clean sans-serif (Inter) for body text, generous spacing throughout
- **Logo**: The uploaded logo will be embedded in the header and footer

## Page Sections

### Navigation Bar
- Fixed minimal header with the Tea Mind logo on the left and clean navigation links on the right
- Transparent over hero, transitions to solid on scroll

### Section 1 — Hero
- Full-width hero with off-white background and subtle animated SVG lines (structured network/intelligence motif in deep green with minimal gold accents)
- Left-aligned headline: *"We think in markets, not in cups."*
- Subheadline and two CTAs: "Explore Programs" (solid green) and "Enter the Society" (outline)
- Generous whitespace, smooth fade-in entrance animations

### Section 2 — About
- Two-column layout: text left, abstract structured line/botanical SVG composition right
- Headline: *"The Intelligence Behind the Industry"*
- Concise paragraph about strategic positioning and market expertise

### Section 3 — Programs
- Clean three-card grid: Professional Formations, Specializations, Advanced Programs
- Each card with title, short description, minimal gold divider, and subtle deep-green hover overlay
- Lots of negative space, no heavy shadows

### Section 4 — The Tea Mind Society
- Full-width section with Midnight Blue (#0F1A2B) background
- Headline: *"The Inner Circle"* with gold accent line
- Short paragraph about selective access and a "Discover the Society" CTA button

### Section 5 — Philosophy Statement
- Centered large serif quote: *"We structure what others consume."*
- Minimal gold underline, generous whitespace

### Footer
- Deep Green (#1C2B22) background
- Clean layout with navigation links, contact info, minimal social icons, and the logo
- Institutional and uncluttered

## Motion & Interaction
- Smooth fade-in animations on scroll for each section
- Subtle parallax on hero background lines
- Refined hover effects on cards and buttons
- All motion restrained and premium-feeling

## Technical Approach
- Single-page React app, fully responsive
- Hero animation via CSS/SVG (performant, no heavy libraries)
- Google Fonts: Playfair Display + Inter
- No backend needed — purely a front-end landing page
