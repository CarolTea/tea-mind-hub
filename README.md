# Tea Mind Hub

Multilingual digital platform built for Tea Mind Hub.

This project represents an earlier stage of my work as a builder, when I was primarily focused on creating websites, digital experiences and structured online platforms before moving deeper into apps and software products.

Tea Mind Hub was developed as a complete web presence with multiple content areas, programs, institutional pages and conversion flows.

## Project overview

The goal was to create a digital hub capable of organizing different parts of the Tea Mind ecosystem within a coherent user experience.

Rather than building a single landing page, the project was structured as a multi-page platform with dedicated experiences for:

* programs;
* institutional content;
* innovations;
* the Neural System;
* partners;
* society and community initiatives;
* events;
* special partner experiences;
* product and conversion pages;
* legal and privacy information.

## Multilingual experience

The platform was designed to support audiences in:

* English
* Portuguese
* Spanish

Routes and content experiences were structured separately for the three languages rather than relying only on automatic browser translation.

Examples include localized pages for programs, institutional content, innovations, events and legal information.

## Product and UX work

My work on the project included thinking through how a broad set of information and initiatives could be organized into a digital experience that remained understandable to the user.

Some of the challenges involved:

* information architecture;
* navigation across multiple sections;
* responsive layouts;
* multilingual user flows;
* visual hierarchy;
* conversion-oriented pages;
* consistency across different types of content;
* integrating institutional and commercial experiences within the same platform.

The project includes:

* route-based page architecture;
* reusable UI components;
* language-specific experiences;
* global query management;
* reusable hooks;
* shared contexts;
* form handling and validation;
* analytics and conversion tracking;
* cookie consent handling;
* testing infrastructure.
